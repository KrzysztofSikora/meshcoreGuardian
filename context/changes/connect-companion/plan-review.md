## Decisions the PR reviewer should look at
- D-4: the 30 s / 5 s handshake bounds, and where the PIN dialog really falls (finding 2).
- D-5: capability minimum ver codes come from firmware comments only, and every row except ver code 13 is `UNVERIFIED`.
- D-7: `expo-sqlite/kv-store` before the S-03 storage foundation (costly; persisted key `companion.last`).
- D-13: Metro stubs `serialport`/`net` as empty modules. The bundle check in Phase 4 is the proof.

## What is good
- The Findings are well grounded: every cited file, line and symbol I checked exists and says what the plan claims. This includes `fixtures.int.test.ts:16`, the device-info offsets 1/60–79 that `withFirmware` relies on, the handshake frames 0–3 of the fixture, the `events.js` `setTimeout(0)`, the dynamic `serialport`/`net` imports, and `mesh-client.ts`, which is already in `stryker.targets`.
- D-13 matches the Expo Metro docs (`resolveRequest` → `{ type: "empty" }`), and the Phase 4 bundle check finds a failure before the e2e phase.
- Phases are ordered by dependency. The adapter enters the app bundle only in Phase 4, the same phase as the Metro fix, so every phase stays green.
- The Phase 2/3 test cases are concrete and include the bounds in fake time ("at 30 s and not before"), no late resolve after a drop, and no auto-reconnect after 10 min.
- Scope stays tight: contacts, remote requests, storage foundation and the settings UI are left out explicitly, and D-5 C rejects probing commands over LoRa.
