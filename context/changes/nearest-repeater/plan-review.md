## Decisions the PR reviewer should look at
- D-2: a new single-row table `contact_sync_origin` that is not in the PRD data model (`prd.md:176`). It is costly (schema) and interacts with finding 1.
- D-1: stores decoded hops, not the raw `out_path_len` byte, while the encoding is `UNVERIFIED`. A wrong decode needs a resync and is not fixed by data migration.

## What is good
- Clear trace from every FR-21 AC to a test or flow: the ranking and tie-break tables, the catalog test that bans "in range"/"online", the AC6 frame assertion and the existing no-location permission test.
- The Findings are grounded and correct. Contact byte offsets match a 148-byte frame. All 31 recorded contacts have path `ff` and lat/lon 0,0, and 27 of them are repeaters, so the "≥ 7 repeaters" fake scenario is feasible from the recorded script.
- The plan applies the lesson "capture context before the first await" with a disconnect test.
- Reusing `runs.start` plus navigation keeps the airtime notice (`run-controller.ts:110`) and the refusal handling in one place.
- The domain stays import-free. The adapter already depends on `@guardian/domain`, so `advertisedPosition` there respects the layer rules.
