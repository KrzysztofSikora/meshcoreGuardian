## Decisions the PR reviewer should look at
- D-2: removing `INTERNET` in the release variant only. It changes the Play listing, and the policy and Data safety answers depend on it.
- D-1: EAS-managed upload keystore and remote versioning. This fixes the signing identity of the Play app.
- D-8: seed shape. Its reason rests on a chart-range feature that does not exist (finding 1).
- Missing costly decision: the privacy policy content and the Data safety answers (finding 2).

## What is good
- Clear split between what the agent does and what a person does (upload, `eas init`, publishing the URL, Data safety). It is grounded in the EAS rule that the first upload must be manual and in Play's two-place policy rule.
- The release-only `INTERNET` removal is backed by the release-APK e2e run and by a debug-manifest check, so Metro dev builds keep working.
- The licence source (source map plus native-module deps) is well argued against `pnpm licenses list`. Native Gradle notices are deferred honestly as `[FOLLOW-UP]`.
- The drift test between the catalog policy and `docs/privacy-policy.md` keeps the in-app copy and the public copy identical.
- The lessons on back stack, list height and full-suite runs after shared layout changes are applied in Phases 2 and 4.
