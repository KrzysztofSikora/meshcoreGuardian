## Decisions the PR reviewer should look at
- D-10: the Android `applicationId` `io.github.krzysztofsikora.guardian` becomes permanent at the first Play upload (S-10). It is the only `costly` decision; it is isolated to `app.json` and the e2e YAMLs.
- D-1: places the native wrappers in `apps/mobile/src/platform/`, against the tech-stack *Architecture sketch*. It fixes where S-02 puts `BleTransport`.
- D-11: release versus debug e2e build contradicts test-plan line 66 (finding 1).
- D-5: ESLint 10 with plugins that declare peer `^9`, which relies on a workaround.

## What is good
- Grounding is unusually solid. The plan verified registry versions, the ble-plx 3.5.1 built-in plugin and its `neverForLocation` behaviour, the ESLint 10 `react/display-name` crash and its workaround, and `node:sqlite` under Vitest 5. The toolchain facts (pnpm 12.9.1, Node 24.21.0, no NDK, AVD `guardian`, Maestro 2.11.0) match this machine.
- The scope is tight. `MeshTransport`, `SqlDriver`, Zustand and Zod are deferred to their owning slices, and D-8 avoids guessing the S-01 interface.
- D-7 gives Stryker a real domain module (FR-06 codes) instead of throwaway code.
- Every phase has gate commands from `CLAUDE.md`. The e2e fallback to a human check follows the roadmap *Unknowns*. The prebuild manifest grep makes C-01, C-02 and "no location" observable.
- D-9 makes a mistyped transport fail loudly, and it has a table test.
