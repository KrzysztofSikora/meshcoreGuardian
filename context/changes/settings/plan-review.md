## Decisions the PR reviewer should look at
- D-12: the cooldown after deletion. The radio is already protected by the session ledger; the open question is whether the button should stay disabled (finding 1).
- D-7: "Delete history" keeps the `devices` row and `dashboard_pinned` (costly, deletes data). Check that this matches the expectation from FR-14 AC3, "deletes a node".
- D-9: what "Clear all data" leaves behind (settings, airtime flag, remembered companion).
- D-1: the persisted settings shape: one versioned JSON blob in kv-store (costly, persisted data).

## What is good
- Every cited symbol checked is real and accurate: `DEFAULT_DIAGNOSTIC_POLICY`, `ATTEMPTS_RANGE`, `OVERRIDE_RANGE_MS`, the `ExportController` `redactionDefault` port with its `true` fallback, `GuestPasswords.remove`/`clearAll` semantics, the private `reload`/`refresh` in `NodesController`, the kv-store's separate `ExpoSQLiteStorage` database, the `diag-no-reply`/`diag-ok` scenarios and the `voltage-history` seed.
- Deleting child rows explicitly in one transaction removes the dependence on `PRAGMA foreign_keys` (the test helper does not enable it). The `DataEraser` port keeps the costly decision in one file.
- Failure paths are spelled out with honest texts and tests: unreadable settings, write failure, partial delete (rows vs secret), busy.
- The e2e flows make the policy visible through existing result texts ("Waited 5 s (your setting)", "up to 1 per check"), and the plan follows the lessons on e2e greps and screenshots.
- The bounds are derived from the domain constants instead of being re-typed.
