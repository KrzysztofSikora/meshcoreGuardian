# Privacy policy: MeshCore Guardian

Effective 18 October 2026. Version 1.0.

## What the app stores

MeshCore Guardian keeps the nodes your companion reports, the results of the diagnostic checks you run, battery readings, your labels and settings. All of it is stored on this phone only.

## Guest passwords

A guest password you enter for a node is kept in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore). It is never written to the app database and never included in an export.

## No network

The app makes no network requests. It has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to talk to the MeshCore companion device you choose. It does not use your location.

## Exports

A report leaves the phone only when you share it yourself. Public keys are shortened by default, and you can change that for each export.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays on the phone until you remove it. In Settings you can delete the history of one node or clear all data. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

The app is a tool for operators of MeshCore radios and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the handling of data changes, this policy is updated and the version and date above change with it.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact e-mail shown on the app's Google Play listing.
